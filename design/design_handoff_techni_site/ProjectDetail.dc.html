A game-reserve build with an estate's rulebook

Royalston is a private game reserve with strict architectural and environmental controls, and House Venter had to meet them at every stage — from material deliveries through a working reserve to finishes signed off against the estate's guidelines.

We built an upmarket double-storey dwelling of roughly 550 m² to the designs of Michael Jack Architects: stacked stone cladding, full-height glazing to the reserve side, and a terraced outdoor living level with pool.

Completed in 2022 at a contract value of R9 million. The client and architect are contactable as references on request.
