ABOUT TECHNI CONSTRUCTION

Thirty-three years of building, still led from site.

Techni Construction was established in 1993 by Wayne Smart and Johan Botha. Both have since retired; the firm they built now delivers residential, commercial and industrial projects up to R200 million across Nelson Mandela Bay and the Eastern Cape.

1993

Founded in Port Elizabeth by builders Wayne Smart and Johan Botha as Smart and Botha Construction.

2000s

Growth into commercial and industrial work; long relationships formed with the region's architects and developers.

2010s

CIDB 7 GB grading achieved — top-tier regional contractor status. Public-sector projects completed alongside private work.

Today

Led by four working directors; 50+ site employees, 6 site managers, 4 office staff. ISO-9001 registration in progress.

SMME & HDI DEVELOPMENT

Specialised works build small businesses too.

Our specialised works and bulk wet trades run through SMME labour-only subcontractors — with a deliberate programme of development, upliftment and skills transfer for historically disadvantaged individuals.

This is not a compliance line. It is how we resource projects, and it is why public-sector and corporate clients keep working with us. We hold a BEE Level 4 rating.
