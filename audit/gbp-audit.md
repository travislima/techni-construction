## Executive summary

Techni Construction's Google Business Profile — the listing with the map, stars
and photos that appears when anyone Googles the company or searches for a
builder nearby — is **working against the business**. The profile appears
unclaimed, its Website button sends searchers to a dead Facebook redirect
instead of the company site, its cover image is a six-year-old photo of a cake,
its most recent review is an unanswered 1★, and in a Maps search from its own
neighbourhood the company ranks **8th of 8 — behind a 4.8★ competitor on the
same street**.

**Overall health: 3 / 16 — Critical.**
(Rubric: eight areas below, each scored 0 = failing, 1 = partial, 2 = healthy.)

## Findings

### 1. Claim and control — CRITICAL
The knowledge panel offers "Own this business?", which Google shows on
listings that aren't currently verified. Two owner-uploaded photos exist, so
the listing was managed at some point and control has likely lapsed. Until
it's reclaimed, nothing else can be fixed reliably — and any member of the
public can "Suggest an edit" that Google may apply.

### 2. The Website button misdirects every click — CRITICAL
The listing's Website button points to the Facebook page
(facebook.com/Techni-Construction-337027756506698), which now redirects to a
generic profile URL. Every searcher who clicks "Website" from Google is sent
to social media — not to the site with the portfolio and contact details. It
also starves the domain of the local-relevance signal Google draws from the
website link.

### 3. Address inconsistency — HIGH
Google says *6 Giulietti Rd, Gqebera, 6011*; the website says *6 & 8 Giulietti
Road, Walmer Dunes Industrial, Walmer, 6070* (*to be confirmed*). Suburb and
postcode both differ, and the map pin reads as sitting on the Gqebera side
rather than in the industrial park. Consistency of **name, address and phone**
across the web (the industry calls this "NAP consistency") is a core local
ranking signal; the phone number, at least, matches everywhere. Email also
drifts by source: admin@ on the website, gregg@ on the ECMBA listing.

### 4. Reviews: weak, stale and unanswered — HIGH
4.1★ from 11 ratings: eight 5★, one 3★, two 1★. The two 1★ ratings drag the
average down by roughly 0.7 stars on this small base. **None of the 11
reviews contains any written text** — so there is no customer sentiment to
analyse or quote (this report says so rather than inventing themes), and no
keyword or trust content for Google to index. No review has an owner
response, including both 1★s. Recency is poor: the newest is ~10 months old
— and it's a 1★ — with a cliff back to reviews 4–7 years old. There is,
effectively, no review-acquisition process.

### 5. Photos: the cover image is a cake — HIGH
Three images: a logo, a "Built on Excellence" banner, and one customer photo
of a branded cake from ~6 years ago. For a contractor targeting projects up
to R200m there are no completed-project photos, no premises, no team, no
equipment, no video. The first visual a searcher sees is the cake.

### 6. Conversion features unused — MEDIUM
No owner-written description beyond a short vision statement mirrored from
Facebook, no Services or Products sections, no Google Posts ever, no Q&A, no
quote link, no attributes. Nearby competitors already display attributes like
"On-site services" and "Online estimates" in the same results.

### 7. Holiday hours unconfirmed — LOW–MEDIUM
Google is warning searchers "National Women's Day might affect these hours."
Holiday hours have never been confirmed, which lowers Google's confidence in
the listing's hours generally.

### 8. An entire second location is missing from Google — OPPORTUNITY
Techni's own Facebook content advertises a **"Gqeberha Sales Centre"** for
bricks and pavers at Unit 3, 25 Kwaford Rd, Struandale, 6390
(+27 41 271 0077). No Google listing exists for it. That's a retail-intent
location — category "Building materials supplier" — currently invisible.

### 9. Third-party data drift — MEDIUM
yep.co.za shows different hours (08:00–17:00) and a lower capacity claim
("up to R100 Mil" vs the site's R200m). Procompare lists "Techni Construction
Cc" positioned around kitchen/bathroom renovations — at odds with the
commercial/industrial brand. ECMBA carries a fax number and a different
email. A separate "Techni Civils" Maps listing (4.5★, 4 reviews) may cause
brand confusion; relationship unconfirmed.

2. Change the Website field to https://www.techniconstruction.co.za; move
   Facebook to social links.

4. Respond professionally to all 11 reviews, starting with the two 1★s.
5. Upload a proper logo and cover image. Retire the cake.
6. Confirm holiday hours for the rest of 2026.

### 30 days

   premises, team, plant.
8. Add secondary categories: General contractor; Commercial construction;
   Building materials supplier (sales centre); Home builder if residential
   remains a focus.

   project management) and set attributes.

    private/public sector experience, project value range, ECMBA membership.
11. Launch the review programme: request at project handover, target 2–4
    written reviews/month (~15 new 5★ reviews moves 4.1 → ~4.6).
12. Seed 5–8 Q&A entries (service area, project sizes, quote process, sectors).

### 90 days

    25 Kwaford Rd, Struandale, 6390) under a building-materials category.

    Connection: align address, hours, capacity claims, email.
